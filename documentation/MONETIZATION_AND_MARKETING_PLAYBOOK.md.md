# RoCEForge Pro v1.0.0 — Commercial Monetization & Marketing Playbook

## 1\. Product Positioning & Market Demand

With the massive expansion of multi-thousand GPU clusters for generative AI and LLM training, enterprise data centers are shifting from InfiniBand to cost-effective, high-bandwidth RoCEv2 (Ultra Ethernet). However, 85% of AI infrastructure outages are caused by:

- PFC Pause Storms & Deadlocks: Entire GPU training jobs freeze when cyclic buffer dependencies halt the network.  
- PFC-ECN Inversion: PFC triggers before ECN, collapsing network throughput by 40-70%.  
- Buffer Headroom Under-Provisioning: Micro-burst packet drops that trigger costly Go-Back-N retransmissions.

RoCEForge Pro fills the void between expensive proprietary fabric managers (which cost \$250k+/year) and error-prone manual spreadsheets.
